Hello! I am Parnika, a research engineer focused on **reinforcement learning, robot imitation learning, and the real-time deployment of learned models on robotic hardware**. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Machine Learning) from the [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca/en/) and [Mila — Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en).

At Mila's [REAL Lab](https://mila.quebec/en), under the supervision of [Prof. Glen Berseth](https://neo-x.github.io), I designed a transformer-based actor-critic architecture with a novel tokenization scheme that trains a **single reinforcement-learning policy across multiple robot morphologies**. By conditioning the policy on BERT-encoded URDF (robot-structure) descriptions, it generalizes to robot embodiments unseen during training — without per-robot retraining ([report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19MFD4Jr_1rQz1EvbjWpictqVTaWjLhgb/view?usp=sharing)). Earlier, I collaborated with [Prof. Shalabh Bhatnagar](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~shalabh/) and [Prof. Raghuram Bharadwaj](https://www.iiitb.ac.in/faculty/raghuram-bharadwaj) at the Indian Institute of Science on attention-based actor-critic methods for constrained cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning, published at **AAMAS 2021**.

More recently, I set out to complement my simulation research with hands-on robot experience: I purchased an **SO-101 robotic arm** and built an end-to-end imitation-learning pipeline — from teleoperated data collection to on-hardware policy deployment — fine-tuning ACT, π0.5, and SmolVLA and evaluating them directly on the arm ([results and failure analysis](/robot-learning/)).

On the industry side, I worked as a Technologist at Wipro Ltd., where I optimized a semantic-segmentation model for real-time scene understanding on an autonomous-vehicle platform — converting it to an INT8-quantized TensorRT engine and achieving a **3× inference speedup on an NVIDIA Jetson ORIN**. Before that, I was a Research Engineer at Mindtree Ltd., implementing research papers for customer use cases across reinforcement learning, NLP and computer vision. I completed my B.Tech. in Computer Science & Engineering at Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, India.

I am keen on growing as an end-to-end robotics engineer across learning, perception, and deployment. I work primarily in Python and PyTorch.
